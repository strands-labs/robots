### Fixed

- **policies/protomotions**: `MotionPlayer` now settles a reference-motion cache's frame count once instead of trusting a declared `num_frames` its channels cannot serve. Every state channel is documented as `[num_frames, ...]`, so a clip states its length several times over; a cache edited by hand (channels trimmed or concatenated, `num_frames` left behind) previously either overran the arrays part-way through playback with a bare `IndexError` - the frame index is clamped to the declared count, and the tracker's future window reads ahead of the playhead - or silently hid the tail and let `save_cache_npz` persist the wrong count. Disagreeing statements are now refused on load with both counts and a remedy named; a consistent cache, and one that omits `num_frames`, are unaffected.
