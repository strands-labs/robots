### Fixed

- **training/rl**: `EmpiricalNormalization` refuses an observation shape it can only broadcast against, at both entry points. A single unbatched `(D,)` observation - the shape `GymSimEnv` and the gymnasium contract hand back - was returned as `(1, D)` and folded as `D` samples of a one-feature stream, advancing `count` by `D` for one observation and collapsing every per-feature mean and std onto a single pooled number inside buffers that persist into the checkpoint. A `(batch, 1)` batch into a wider normalizer broadcast the same way, writing one column's statistics into every feature. The refusal names the shape read, the shape received, and the call that batches a single observation, and is checked before `count` moves, so a rejected batch no longer skews the `count_x / count` update rate of the batches after it.
