### Fixed

- **mesh**: the `pose` topic now reports the rotation its SE(3) matrix encodes for every orientation. The quaternion was built from the trace branch of Shepperd's method alone, which cannot serve `trace <= 0` - exactly a rotation of 120 degrees or more, 61% of SO(3) under the uniform measure - and the identity quaternion was substituted there, so a robot that had turned to face back the way it came published `theta` = pi beside a `quat` meaning no rotation at all, as a valid unit quaternion no consumer could question. All four branches are now taken, the result is normalized (so an odometry- or SLAM-derived block that is only approximately orthonormal still yields a unit quaternion instead of one whose length carries the drift onto the wire) and sign-canonicalized to `w >= 0`.
