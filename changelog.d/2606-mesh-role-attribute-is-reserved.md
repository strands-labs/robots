### Fixed

- **mesh/iot**: `provision_robot` / `provision_operator` no longer merge a caller-supplied `strands-mesh-role` Thing attribute. That attribute is the key the deployed e-stop fan-out routes on - it enumerates Things and publishes `{"action": "stop"}` only to those labelled `robot` - so `setdefault` letting a caller's value win silently excluded that robot from every fleet-wide stop while provisioning reported success, the Thing existed, its certificate worked and it obeyed `cmd`. The key is now refused before boto3 is resolved, on the same rung as the `thing_name` charset and the `allow_estop_publish` flag, so a refused call provisions nothing. The `attributes` docstring also stated a three-key budget as the caller's while this function spends one of AWS's three itself, and read 800 characters as a dict total rather than the per-value bound `CreateThing` applies.
