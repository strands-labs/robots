### Fixed

`add_robot` now reports the world position the robot actually compiled at instead of echoing the requested `position` back. `position` is the attach frame's translation, which MuJoCo composes with the `pos` the model's root body declares, so for the 30 single-root registry robots that declare a non-zero root pose the reported vector named a place the robot was not - `position=[0, 0, 0.4]` on a Unitree Go2 puts its base at `z=0.845`, and up to 1.4 m out on a JVRC. The result now names the measured placement, plus the request and the model's own offset whenever the two differ; a zero-offset arm and a robot with more than one root body read exactly as before. The offset semantics are documented in `add_robot`, on the `describe()` surface and in the world-building guide, beside the contrast with `add_object`, whose `position` does place its body at that world point.
