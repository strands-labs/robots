### Fixed: `start_recording` on a machine that cannot render reported success for a camera no frame would carry. On headless Linux without EGL or OSMesa the sim skips camera frames, yet the dataset schema still declared `observation.images.default`, so the first `add_frame` of the rollout failed with "Missing features" after the recording had been reported as started with one camera. `start_recording` now refuses up front, before any dataset is created or wiped, naming the cameras it cannot capture and `cameras=[]` as the way to record joint state and actions only on that machine.
