### Fixed

- **mesh/security**: the built-in `policy_type` allowlist now admits every policy provider `registry/policies.json` ships, including each provider's aliases and shorthands. 22 of the 28 spellings `create_policy` resolves were previously refused on the mesh `tell()` path and by the Device Connect drivers, so a provider that worked locally was unreachable remotely unless the operator set the broad `STRANDS_MESH_POLICY_TYPE_ALLOW` grant. Widening the provider vocabulary relaxes no other gate: `policy_host`, `server_address`, `pretrained_name_or_path` and `model_path` are still validated for every payload, an unregistered provider name is still refused, and a runtime-registered provider is still not admitted on the wire. A guard test now fails CI when a provider is added to the registry without the corresponding allowlist entry.
