### Fixed

- **tools**: an operator's human-in-the-loop reply is now recorded in the local safety audit log at every gate, not only at `robot_mesh`. `use_ros` (a `publish` / `service_call` / `action_send_goal` aimed at a safety-critical graph surface) and `lerobot_train` (the blocked `extra_flags`) wrote no audit row and emitted no log record on either outcome, so a declined `/cmd_vel` publish or `output_dir` override left no trace that a gate had fired, let alone why an operator refused - and every gate accepts a canonical affirmative only, so a reply carrying a reason is always a decline and that row is the only place the reason survives. One owner now writes the row (`strands_robots.tools._hitl_audit.log_operator_response`) so its wording cannot differ between two gates writing to the same log; `robot_mesh`'s rows are byte-identical. The reply still never reaches the model.
