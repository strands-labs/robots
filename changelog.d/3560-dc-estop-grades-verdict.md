### Fixed: `robot_mesh(action="emergency_stop")` over Device Connect reported every device as stopped as long as its `stop` RPC returned a reply, so a device whose allowlist refused the caller, or whose `stop_policy` could not halt a rollout, was counted in "N/N devices stopped" under a successful status. The tool now reads each reply with the same rule the built-in mesh path uses: a device that reports it did not stop turns the result into an error naming that device and its answer, and the refusal is logged at CRITICAL. An unreachable device is still a gap in the count rather than a claimed stop.
