### Fixed

- **tools/lerobot_camera**: `color_mode` and `rotation` are now held to their documented vocabularies at the dispatch boundary, like the seven numeric options beside them. Neither was ever refused downstream: an unrecognised `color_mode` selected `BGR` and an unrecognised `rotation` selected `NO_ROTATION`, both reporting success. A `color_mode` the driver reads as `BGR` is delivered in that channel order while the capture path converts what it is handed with `COLOR_RGB2BGR` regardless, so a red object was written blue under an "Image Capture Success!" summary; a trailing space (`"rgb "`) or a transposed pair (`"RBG"`) was enough to reach it. Both spellings stay case-insensitive, so only the values that used to be silently replaced are refused, and `_create_camera` now resolves through the same module-level vocabularies the gate validates against.
