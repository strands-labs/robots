### Fixed: `robot_mesh(action="stop")` over the mesh reported every envelope as a success

`Mesh.send` answers a single-target stop with one of three shapes - the peer's response carrying the handler's own verdict, the peer's own `type: error` for a lockout or authorization rejection, or `send`'s own `timeout` / precondition error - and the `stop` branch read none of them: only a *raised* `send` reached the error path. A refused stop, a lockout rejection and a stop nothing answered all came back as `status="success"` with an `ok=True` audit row, while the receiving peer had already audited `command_refused` for the same turn. The branch now grades the envelope: a handler that reports it did not stop (the rule the fleet-wide `emergency_stop` reads), a peer-level `type: error`, a `send` precondition error, or no answer inside the budget each return an error naming the peer and its answer, audit `ok=False`, and log at CRITICAL. A response that reports it halted, or one that carries no verdict either way, is still a success.
