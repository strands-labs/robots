### Fixed

- **simulation**: `run_policy` reports the policy-binding flags and policy-load telemetry at every episode count. The `n_episodes > 1` aggregate was built separately from the single-rollout payload and carried neither group, so the health gate `run_policy`'s own docstring tells a caller to read - `positional_fallback_used`, `generic_state_keys_used`, `missing_state_keys_used` - resolved to its healthy default on the shape that collects a dataset: the same policy on the same robot for the same horizon read degraded at `n_episodes=1` and healthy at `n_episodes=3`, both `status="success"`. `policy_load_cache_hit` and `policy_resident_rss_mb` are documented for a loop specifically (a cache miss on episode 2+ means the caller rebuilt the policy; flat RSS means the model stayed resident), so the multi-episode payload is the one that has to carry them. The aggregate now carries those six plus the identity fields, read off the one policy object the episodes shared - the way `eval_policy` and `evaluate_benchmark` already report them for their own multi-episode aggregates. Per-episode action health stays in the `episodes` records, and both the docstring and the simulation overview now say where to read it.
