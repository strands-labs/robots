### Fixed: a label sidecar record this build cannot read is refused, not reinterpreted

`read_labels` is the boundary for an `episode_labels.json` sidecar this build did not write, and it already refused an unparseable document and an unknown `schema_version` rather than project one onto this version's field meanings. That rule stopped at the document. Inside each record, a `deterministic.success` spelled `"false"` is truthy, so a deterministically FAILED episode cleared `filter_episodes(require_success=True)` and entered the training set; a `judge.quality` outside `QUALITY_GRADES` reached that same filter as `tuple.index(x): x not in tuple` - naming neither the file, the episode, the field nor the vocabulary - and reached `measure_agreement` as a silently understated agreement figure, the number that decides whether a judge is trusted to filter training data, wrong in the direction that makes a sound judge look unsound.

`read_labels` now grades every record through one owner, so every reader reaches the same verdict about the same bytes rather than re-deriving one, and the refusal names the sidecar, the episode and the vocabulary. The split is the one `strands_robots.transforms.provenance` already applies at both ends while citing this module's writers for it: the keys a reader turns into a verdict are held to their domains, and the descriptive keys are carried through untouched. Nothing the module's own writers produce changes.
