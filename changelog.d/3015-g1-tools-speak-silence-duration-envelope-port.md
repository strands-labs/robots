### Added: `strands_robots.tools.g1.g1_speak_silence_duration_envelope` lookup pair

Ports the neon bidi `g1_speak` verb's turn-end silence-duration hint (`silence_duration_ms = 700` in `cagataycali/neon-the-g1/tools/g1_speak.py`) into a read-only agent-facing lookup pair — `g1_list_speak_silence_duration_envelope()` surfaces the observed 700 ms default, and `g1_speak_silence_duration_ms_admits(silence_duration_ms=...)` grades a caller-supplied duration against the shared `positive_count_error` domain the strands bidi turn-end detector implicitly reads. Refusals cite `refs strands-labs/robots#358` and quote a module-local text rather than borrowing the motion-FSM `7404` rc, keeping the remedy on the bidi speak surface the write belongs on. Import is audio-stack-free (no `pywebrtc_audio` / `pyaudio` submodule loads at import time) so a caller planning against a target turn-end latency budget on a host without the audio extra installed still reads the envelope back verbatim. Twin of the merged `g1_speak_vad_envelope` (sensitivity threshold) and `g1_bidi_audio_stream_delay_envelope` (AEC alignment window) — three modules, three disjoint bidi speak dimensions each with its own remedy path.
