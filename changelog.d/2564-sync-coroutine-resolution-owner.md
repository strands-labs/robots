### Fixed

- **policies**: `Policy.get_actions_sync` now resolves through `strands_robots._async_utils`, the one owner of "resolve a policy coroutine in a sync context", instead of building a private `ThreadPoolExecutor` per call - so the offload branch a notebook or any async host lands in reuses that module's single worker rather than starting and joining one OS thread per call. In the same owner, the branch guard now covers only the running-loop check: with the offload inside it, a `RuntimeError` raised by the awaited coroutine was read as the guard's own "no running loop" verdict, and the caller received `asyncio.run() cannot be called from a running event loop` in place of what the coroutine said - the whole family, `NotImplementedError` and `RecursionError` with it.
