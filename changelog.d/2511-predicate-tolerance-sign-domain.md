### Fixed

- **simulation/predicates**: a negative tolerance in the predicate DSL is refused when the clause compiles instead of producing a clause that can never be satisfied. Every tolerance is compared against a distance, an absolute difference or a squared magnitude - never negative - so `{predicate: distance_less_than, threshold: -0.3}` was permanently `False` while reading as a *wider* bound, and a `run_policy(stop_when=...)` rollout built on it spent its whole step budget reporting an honest miss under `status="success"`. `body_upright` and `base_tipped` already refused `tol < 0`; the domain now covers the whole family (`distance_less_than`'s `threshold`, `body_inside`'s `xy_tol`/`z_tol`, `body_on`'s `xy_tol`), the nested `staged_reward` calls, and any predicate added later that names its tolerance the same way - the pour predicates' `xy_tol` / `z_tol` are covered with no edit here, because the domain is read from the parameter name rather than from a table of predicates. Signed params are untouched - `body_on`'s `z_offset`, `base_velocity`'s `vx` and `body_below_z`'s `z` still accept a negative value.
