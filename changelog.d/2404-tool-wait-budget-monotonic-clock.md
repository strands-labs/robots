### Fixed

- **tools**: a wait budget an agent-callable tool publishes as a parameter (`use_ros(timeout=...)`, `use_rtps(timeout=...)`, `lerobot_camera(preview_duration=...)`, `serial_tool`'s monitor window, the GR00T service-readiness poll, the rosbridge reconnect wait) is measured on `time.monotonic()`. It was measured on `time.time()`, so a wall-clock step mid-wait -- an NTP correction, a `date -s`, a resume from suspend -- moved the deadline by the size of the step: forward, the wait ended early with the work still in flight and an arriving message was reported as a timeout; backward, it ran past the caller's budget. In `use_ros` that also put a single `action_send_goal` deadline on two clocks and could truncate the cancel sent on expiry, which needs the executor pumped to leave the process. Absolute stamps are unchanged: `serial_tool` still stamps each returned monitor record with the wall clock.
